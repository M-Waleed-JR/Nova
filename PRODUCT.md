# Nova Electronics

## Product Vision
A premium, high-performance electronics store targeting tech enthusiasts, creators, and professionals. The brand feels futuristic, innovative, and reliable.

## Key Values
- **Precision**: Pixel-perfect UI and technical details.
- **Innovation**: Using 3D and motion to showcase cutting-edge gear.
- **Exclusivity**: A "boutique" feel for high-end gadgets.

## Success Metrics
- High engagement with product showcases.
- Low bounce rate due to immersive visual storytelling.
- Accessibility for all users despite heavy visual effects.
